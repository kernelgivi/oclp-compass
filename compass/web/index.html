      POLICY, ASSUMPTIONS & REFERENCES

      Understand the result.

      Compatibility rules decide what is eligible. Preference weights decide what ranks highest. Owner reports have a small, bounded influence.

      The ranking in plain language

      Compass starts with a hand-reviewed model profile. It estimates how memory, storage, OS overhead, and graphics patching may affect responsiveness, then combines that estimate with OS recency and maintenance ease.

      These constants have not been fitted to benchmarks. A high score does not establish that an OS is secure, an application will work, or your Mac will run smoothly.

      How owner reports influence the estimate

      Only exact-model reports for the target major OS are matched. One author contributes once per model and OS. Old reports and incomplete or different hardware configurations carry less weight. Eight neutral pseudo-observations temper small samples.

      Adjustment = 8 × weighted sentiment / (8 + effective sample)

      Contradictions are shown, not averaged away from view. Confidence stays “limited” for anecdotal reports. Reports cannot make an excluded version eligible.
